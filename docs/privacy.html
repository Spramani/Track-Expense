Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At TrackExpense, we believe your personal financial data should remain strictly confidential. We are committed to an offline-first architecture that honors your privacy, secures your financial logs, and complies fully with Apple's App Store Guidelines.

1. Information We Collect

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or financial records. The information handled by TrackExpense includes:

  • Financial Entries: Transaction amounts, categories, currency identifiers, payment accounts, merchant notes, and balances you log (Income, Expense, Get/Receivables, and Will Give/Payables).
  • Account & Collaboration: If you sign in using Apple ID or Email, your display name and email address are used solely for account authorization and trip group bill splitting.
  • Trip Group Records: Shared expenses and minimum-transfer settlements you create with invited friends or roommates.
  • Receipt Photos & OCR: Photos or attachments you voluntarily capture for expense record-keeping. Text extraction via Apple Vision OCR is performed on-device.

2. Data Storage & Security

All financial logs are stored locally using encrypted on-device storage. When connected to the internet, entries sync over secure TLS 1.3 encryption to your private cloud storage. We support optional Face ID and Touch ID biometric authentication so only you can unlock the application on your iPhone.

3. Device Permissions

TrackExpense requests device permissions strictly when needed for core features:

  • Camera & Photo Library: To scan or attach receipts to your transactions and allow setting an optional avatar.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: To verify your biometric identity upon app launch if App Lock is enabled.
  • Notifications: To deliver optional reminders for category budget thresholds and scheduled recurring payments.

4. Third-Party Services & Ad-Free Guarantee

We do not integrate with third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or behavioral tracking SDKs. Any crash diagnostic monitoring is strictly anonymized and aggregated to help fix unexpected errors and optimize performance.

5. Your Rights & Instant Data Deletion

You retain full ownership of your data at all times. You can export your data anytime via CSV or PDF statement. You may also permanently delete your account and all associated cloud entries via Profile → Delete Account & Data. Once deleted, your records are purged immediately and permanently.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy practices, please contact our support team at support@trackexpense.app or ruchitaramani.ios@gmail.com.
