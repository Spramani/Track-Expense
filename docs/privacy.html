Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At TrackExpense, we believe your personal financial data should remain strictly yours. We are committed to building an experience that honors your privacy, secures your financial logs, and complies fully with Apple's App Store Guidelines.

1. Information We Collect

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or financial data. The information handled by TrackExpense includes:

  • Financial Entries: Transaction amounts, categories, dates, merchant notes, and account balances you choose to log.
  • Account & Profile: If you sign in using Apple ID or Email, your display name and email address are used solely for account authentication and group collaboration.
  • Group Expenses: Shared bills and group settlement records you create with invited friends or family members.
  • Receipt Photos: Photos or attachments you voluntarily upload for expense record-keeping.

2. Data Storage & Security

All financial records are encrypted both in transit (using modern TLS protocols) and securely stored. We utilize Apple's secure Keychain for sensitive authentication tokens and support Face ID / Touch ID biometric authentication so only you can unlock the app on your device.

3. Device Permissions

TrackExpense requests device permissions only when explicitly needed for core functionality:

  • Camera / Photo Library: To scan or attach receipts to your transactions and allow setting a custom profile avatar.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: To verify your identity when opening the app if app lock is enabled.
  • Notifications: To deliver optional reminders for budget limits and recurring bill payments.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate with third-party advertising networks or data brokers. Analytics or crash monitoring (if enabled) is strictly anonymized and aggregated to help diagnose bugs and improve app stability.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You retain full ownership of your data at all times. You can export your data, edit transactions, or permanently delete your account and all associated records directly from within the app settings. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy practices, please contact our support team at ruchitaramani.ios@gmail.com.
