Support & Help Center

Have questions, need help, or want to suggest a new feature? We are always here for you.

How do I log a transaction quickly?

Tap the + button at the bottom navigation. Enter your amount, select a category, add optional notes or receipt photos, and tap Save. It takes under 3 seconds!

Can I split bills with multiple friends?

Yes! Under the Groups tab, create a trip or roommate group, log shared expenses, and TrackExpense will compute the exact balances so you know who owes whom.

How does multi-currency conversion work?

TrackExpense fetches live exchange rates so you can record expenses while traveling abroad and view your unified net balance converted to your home currency.
