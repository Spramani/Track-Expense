Support & Help Center

Got questions about features, currency rates, or bill splitting? We are here for you.

How do I log a transaction?

Tap the floating + button on the Dashboard or Transactions screen. Choose your transaction type: Income (money received), Expense (money spent), Get (money lent to someone to be repaid), or Will Give (money you borrowed that you owe). Enter the amount, choose a category, pick a payment method, and tap Save Transaction in under 3 seconds.

How does multi-currency conversion work?

TrackExpense supports real-time multi-currency conversions across USD, EUR, INR, GBP, AED, JPY, and more. When you choose your Default Currency in Profile, all balances, charts, and transaction lists automatically convert to that currency while keeping original foreign amounts intact.

Does TrackExpense work completely offline?

Yes! TrackExpense is designed offline-first. Every transaction, budget update, and trip split is saved to your device immediately without needing internet. Whenever you connect, local entries synchronize securely with your cloud storage in the background.

How does Bill Split & Trip Group settlement work?

Under the Groups tab, create a group (e.g., 'Goa Trip 🏖️', 'Apartment Rent 🏠') and add members. Log shared bills and specify who paid. TrackExpense computes the smart minimum-transfer settlement matrix so everyone knows exactly who owes whom, and you can record settlements with a single tap.

Can I scan bills and paper receipts automatically?

Yes! When adding a transaction, tap 'Attach receipt photo' to capture or select a photo. TrackExpense's on-device Apple Vision OCR automatically analyzes the image, detects the total amount, and pre-fills it for you.

How do I export PDF financial reports or CSV files?

Go to the Report tab to generate an executive PDF statement with clean tables and category summaries for any monthly, yearly, or custom range. You can also export a complete CSV file from Profile for Excel, Google Sheets, or Apple Numbers.
