Help & Support Center

Find answers to frequently asked questions or get in touch directly with the developer team.

How do I log a transaction?

Tap the floating + button on the Dashboard or Transactions screen. Choose your transaction type:
• Income: Money received (salary, dividends, freelance, gifts)
• Expense: Money spent (groceries, dining, travel, shopping)
• Get (Receivable): Money lent to someone to be repaid back to you
• Will Give (Payable): Money you borrowed that you owe to someone

Enter the amount, choose a category, pick a payment method (Cash, Bank/UPI, Credit Card), and tap Save Transaction. It records in under 3 seconds!

How does multi-currency conversion work?

TrackExpense supports automatic real-time and local multi-currency conversions across USD ($), EUR (€), INR (₹), GBP (£), AED (د.إ), JPY (¥), CAD, AUD, and more.

In your Profile, select your Default Currency. Your Net Balance, Total Income, and Total Expense dynamically convert to this home currency. When traveling, transactions keep their original currency intact, and detail pages display both your converted amount and original amount side-by-side.

Does TrackExpense work completely offline?

Yes! TrackExpense is built offline-first. Every transaction, budget limit, and trip split is saved to your iPhone instantly without requiring an active internet connection. As soon as your device reconnects, all local entries seamlessly synchronize with your secure cloud storage in the background.

How do I create and manage budgets?

Go to the Budget section and tap Set Budget. Select a category (Food & Dining, Travel, Shopping, Bills), enter your spending limit, and pick Monthly or a Custom Date range.

TrackExpense alerts you as you approach 80% and when you exceed your limit. You can also view unbudgeted spending to identify where unmonitored money goes.

How does Bill Split & Trip Group settlement work?

Navigate to Bill Split or Trip Groups to split shared expenses with friends or roommates:
1. Create a trip or shared group (e.g., Goa Trip 🏖️, Apartment Rent 🏠) and add members.
2. Log shared expenses and specify who paid and how the cost is divided (equally or custom).
3. TrackExpense automatically computes the smart minimum-transfer settlement matrix so everyone knows exactly who owes whom, and you can record settlements with a single tap.

Can I scan bills and receipts automatically?

Yes! When adding a transaction, tap Attach receipt photo to capture or select a photo from your library. TrackExpense's on-device Apple Vision OCR automatically analyzes the image, detects the total amount, and pre-fills it for you while saving the receipt image for tax and warranty reference.

How do I export PDF financial reports or CSV files?

TrackExpense provides two flexible export formats:
• PDF Reports: Go to the Report section, select your timeframe (Monthly, Yearly, or Custom Range), and generate an executive PDF statement with clean tables and visual category breakdowns.
• CSV Export: In Profile, tap Export Transactions (CSV) to generate a spreadsheet file compatible with Microsoft Excel, Apple Numbers, and Google Sheets.

How do I manage banks, cards, and payment sources?

Go to Profile → Payment Sources. You can configure Bank Accounts, UPI IDs, Cash Wallets, and Credit Cards. Each transaction can be tagged with its payment method to track spending across different accounts.

How do I protect my app with Face ID or Touch ID?

In the Profile screen under Security & Privacy, toggle Face ID / Touch ID. When enabled, TrackExpense requires biometric verification whenever you open the app or return from the background, keeping your finances completely confidential.

How do I delete my account and data?

In compliance with Apple App Store guidelines, you have total ownership of your data. Scroll to the bottom of the Profile screen and tap Delete Account & Data. Confirming will permanently and irreversibly erase all your records, receipt photos, budgets, and cloud account credentials.
