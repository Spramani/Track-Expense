Support & FAQ

Find answers to common questions or reach out directly to our team.

How do I log a new transaction?

Tap the + button in the bottom navigation bar. Enter the transaction amount, select an expense category (or income), add an optional merchant note or receipt photo, and tap Save. It logs in under 3 seconds!

How does multi-currency tracking work?

TrackExpense allows you to record expenses in multiple global currencies (USD, EUR, INR, GBP, JPY, and more). The app automatically converts your transactions into your primary base currency using live exchange rates, giving you a crystal-clear unified net worth.

How does group bill splitting work?

Navigate to the Groups tab to create a group for roommates, trips, or dining. Add expenses and choose whether to split equally or by percentage. TrackExpense automatically calculates who owes whom and lets you record settlements seamlessly.

Is my financial data secure and private?

Yes, 100%. We believe financial privacy is sacred. We never sell or share your records with third-party advertisers. You can also enable Face ID / Touch ID biometric lock in Settings to prevent unauthorized access.

Can I attach photos and receipts to transactions?

Yes. While adding or editing an expense, tap the camera or photo gallery icon to capture or upload a paper receipt. Receipts are stored securely with your transaction for easy tax and warranty lookups.

How do I delete my account or reset my data?

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account & Data. Confirming this action will immediately and irreversibly erase all your records, budgets, and group associations.
